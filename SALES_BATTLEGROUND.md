# LexAI Sales Battleground Cards

> **For:** Sales team, customer success, and competitive deals  
> **Use case:** Objection handling during demos, RFP responses, and competitive displacement

## How to Use These Cards

Each competitor has:
- **The Hook** — opening line to reframe the conversation
- **LexAI Wins** — bulletproof differentiators (lead with these)
- **The Trap** — questions to ask that expose competitor weaknesses
- **Objection Handler** — responses when they mention the competitor
- **When to Walk** — deals where you will likely lose; don't waste cycles

> "Harvey is built for AmLaw 100 firms with $175,000 annual budgets. If you're a solo or small firm, or if you practice outside the US/UK, you're paying for features you'll never use and jurisdictions they don't cover."

### LexAI Wins
- **Price:** $300/lawyer/mo published vs. Harvey's quote-only opaque pricing
- **Free tier:** 7-day full access vs. Harvey's no free trial
- **Jurisdictions:** 291 including Bermuda, Caribbean, CCJ, Privy Council vs. Harvey's US/UK only
- **Bar exam prep:** 9 tracks vs. Harvey's none
- **Self-learning AI:** Fine-tunes from your corrections vs. Harvey's static prompting
- **Solo-friendly:** Built for solos first vs. Harvey's enterprise-only focus

### The Trap (Questions to Ask)
1. "What's your firm's annual legal tech budget? If it's under $50K, Harvey won't even return your call."
2. "Do you practice in Bermuda, the Caribbean, or any Commonwealth jurisdiction? Harvey has zero coverage there."
3. "How many lawyers are in your firm? Harvey's median contract is $175K/year — does that fit your budget?"

### Objection Handler
**Prospect:** "We're considering Harvey."
**Response:** "Harvey is excellent if you're a 500-lawyer US firm with a dedicated IT team and a six-figure budget. ### When to Walk
- AmLaw 100 firm with $200K+ budget and purely US/UK practice
- Prospect who says "our managing partner already approved Harvey"
- Firm that requires Westlaw/Lexis integration and won't consider alternatives

## Universal Objection Handlers

### "We need to verify citations / Shepard's / KeyCite"
> "For US Supreme Court and critical appellate citations, we recommend using Lexis or Westlaw as a secondary check — just like you would with any research tool. LexAI's strength is speed, global reach, and AI-powered analysis. For the 80% of research that isn't citation-critical, LexAI is faster and cheaper. For the 20% that is, use us for drafting and analysis, then verify citations in your existing tool."

### "We already have [Competitor X]"
> "Most LexAI customers already have another legal tool. LexAI isn't a replacement — it's a complement that handles everything else. Use [Competitor] for their strength; use LexAI for [Bermuda/Caribbean/drafting/litigation prediction/bar prep/self-learning]. At $300/month, the incremental value is immediate."

### "Your AI might hallucinate"
> "All AI legal tools have hallucination risk — including Harvey, CoCounsel, and ChatGPT. LexAI mitigates this by:
1. Grounding outputs in 4M+ real cases from CourtListener and Harvard CAP
2. Confidence scoring on every analysis
3. Self-learning that improves accuracy with your corrections
4. Clear disclaimers that AI assists — not replaces — lawyer judgment
We recommend our 7-day free trial so you can test accuracy on your actual cases."

### "We need Microsoft Word integration"
> "Word integration is on our Q3 roadmap and in active development. In the meantime, our web platform handles contract drafting, analysis, and export to Word format. Most users find our web interface faster for initial drafting, then finalize in Word. Would you like to be notified when the Word add-in launches?"

### "We need enterprise security certifications"
> "LexAI is SOC 2 Type II certified, ISO 27001 certified, GDPR compliant, and CCPA compliant. We use 256-bit TLS encryption, device fingerprinting, and one-time download tokens. Our security whitepaper is available on request. We're happy to complete your security questionnaire or join a call with your IT team."
